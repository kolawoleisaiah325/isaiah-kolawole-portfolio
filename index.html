01 / ABOUT METHE PERSON BEHIND THE CODE

Curiosity first.
Then the code.

I’m Isaiah Kolawole, a 400-level student at Nile University and an ML/AI engineer exploring how data and software can solve real problems.

My interest in AI started with a simple idea: software could do more than follow instructions—it could learn from examples. That curiosity grew into experimenting, building, and turning “what if?” questions into working projects.

Today, I’m putting that curiosity into practice through forecasting, AI tools, and computer vision. I like getting the data right, testing ideas honestly, and building things that people can understand and use.
